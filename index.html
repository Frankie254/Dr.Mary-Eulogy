Eulogy of the Late Dr. Mary

Introduction

We gather today with heavy hearts, yet with gratitude, to celebrate the life of our beloved sister, wife, mother, daughter, doctor, colleague and friend, Dr. Mary Nekesa Kathuri Gakono. Dr. Mary passed away on 28th September 2026 at Karira Mission Hospital, where she was receiving treatment in the Intensive Care Unit. Dr. Mary was a remarkable woman whose life was defined by love, compassion, dedication and service to others. To her patients, she was a doctor who cared deeply. To her colleagues, she was a professional who was committed to her work.

To her friends, she was a kind and dependable companion. And to her family, she was so much more, a loving wife, a devoted mother, a caring daughter, a cherished sister and a beloved aunt. Her departure has left a deep void in our family and in the many lives she touched. Yet, as we mourn her passing, we also give thanks to God for the years we shared with her, for the love she gave so freely, and for the memories she leaves behind.

Early Life

Dr. Mary was born on 5th July 1981 at Lugulu Mission Hospital in Bungoma County. She was born to the late John Sakini Wamalwa, her father, and Margaret Nanjala Wamalwa, her mother. She was also lovingly raised with the care and guidance of her late stepmother, Angella Wamalwa, who played an important role in her life and upbringing.

She grew up surrounded by a large and loving family, alongside her brothers and sisters: Dalian Wamalwa, George Wamalwa, Mourine Wamalwa, Ian Fredrick Wamalwa, Alfred Wamalwa, the late Patrick Wamalwa, Richard Wamalwa, Protus Wamalwa, Rose Wamalwa, Catherine Wamalwa, Mary Wamalwa, Jack Wamalwa, Agnes Wamalwa, Edward Wamalwa and Bonnie Wamalwa.

She was also surrounded by other mothers and extended family members who played an important role in her upbringing, including Irene and Eunice, as well as her uncles Joshua and Peter.

As an aunt, Mary was deeply loved by many nieces and nephews, including Morris, Martin, Eric, Teva, Simon, Editor, Charles, Purity, Pius, Denis, Francis, Harrison, Christine, Jimpaul, Phides, Anne, Regina, Alex, Madrine, Ian, Edel, Maccabees, Adrian, Matthew and Winfred, among many others.

Education

Dr. Mary began her schooling at Matisi Primary School and went on to Luuya Girls High School, where she completed her secondary education in 2000. Driven by a passion for medicine and service to humanity, she joined Kenya Medical Training College, Kimilili, and in 2007 completed her Diploma in Clinical Medicine. She did not stop there. She later enrolled at Kenyatta University to pursue a degree in Medicine.

Her Professional Journey

Dr. Mary dedicated her working life to healing others. She served at Kimilili Hospital, Busia Level 5 Hospital, Kitale Hospital and Buvori Clinic, and later ventured into business.

For her, medicine was never just a job. She treated patients with compassion and gave her time, knowledge and energy to all who needed her. Many people were treated and healed by her hands, and their testimonies will continue to live among us. Her colleagues will remember her and her patients as the doctor who gave them hope.

Marriage and Family

In 2011, Dr. Mary married David Kathuri Gakono. Their marriage was blessed with two children, Jemima Gracious, aged 9, and Joshua Zion, aged 7.

She was a devoted wife and a loving mother who cherished her husband, her children, her relatives and her neighbours. To her husband, she was a partner, a companion, a source of strength. To her children, she was comfort, encouragement and strength.

Her Character

Dr. Mary was loving, caring and hardworking. She was generous, valued family, and believed in helping others. She had a special way of making people feel cared for. She was also a woman of determination, and whatever she committed herself to, she worked hard to accomplish. Above all, she had a heart for people.

Her Faith

Dr. Mary was baptized in the Pentecostal Church in 2008. In 2011 she embraced the Catholic faith, and she was baptized on 26th September 2016. Her faith remained an important part of her journey. As Christians, we find comfort in the words of Scripture:

“I have fought the good fight, I have finished the race, I have kept the faith.”
— 2 Timothy 4:7

Her earthly journey, though shorter than we wished, was a life of purpose, service and love.

Her Final Journey

In June 2026, Dr. Mary began to feel unwell. She was taken to a number of facilities, including Haven Hospital, Kenyatta National Hospital, Ruai Family Hospital and Karira Mission Hospital. On 28th September 2026, while in the ICU, she succumbed to her illness at the age of 45 years.

Her passing came as a great shock and has left her family, friends and colleagues deeply heartbroken. We prayed for her recovery and hoped she would come home. But God’s ways are not our ways. Today we face the difficult task of saying goodbye to someone we never imagined we would lose so soon.

The Legacy She Leaves

Dr. Mary leaves behind her beloved husband, David Kathuri Gakono; her children, Jemima and Joshua; her mother, Margaret Nanjala Wamalwa; her brothers and sisters; her nephews and nieces, relatives, colleagues and friends; and the many people whose lives she touched through her profession and kindness.

Her greatest legacy is the people she loved and the lives she touched. Her children will carry her legacy. Her family will carry her memories. Her colleagues will remember her service. And her patients will remember the doctor who gave them hope and compassion. Though she is no longer with us, the love she gave us cannot be buried.

To Her Children

Jemima and Joshua, your mother loved you deeply. She dreamed of watching you grow, succeed and become responsible adults. Although she cannot walk every step of the journey with you, everything she taught you will stay within you.

Remain strong. Love one another. Take care of each other. Work hard and live lives that would make your mother proud. Whenever you miss her, remember that you were deeply loved.

To Her Husband

David, we stand with you in your grief. We commit to walking with you and the children in the days ahead.

Tributes

Tribute from Her Husband

My beloved wife, Mary,

Today, I stand before our family, friends and loved ones with a heart full of sorrow, trying to say goodbye to the woman with whom I shared my life.

Mary was more than my wife. She was my companion, my friend, my confidant and the mother of our children. She stood beside me through the different seasons of our lives, sharing in our joys, carrying us through difficult moments and giving our family the strength and love that only she could give.

She was a hardworking woman who never feared responsibility. She cared deeply for her family and was always willing to sacrifice her own comfort for the wellbeing of those she loved. Her greatest joy was seeing her family happy and moving forward.

As a mother, she gave our children love, guidance, protection and hope. She wanted the best for them and worked hard to give them a good foundation in life. Her love for them was evident in everything she did.

Mary also had a heart that extended far beyond her immediate family. She cared for many people and stood in as a mother to those who needed her. Many will remember her kindness, generosity, encouragement and willingness to help.

My dear Mary, losing you has left a space in my life that words cannot describe. There are conversations we will never have again, moments we will never share again and memories that I will carry for the rest of my life.

I promise to do my best to take care of our children and to preserve the values you instilled in them. They will always know how much their mother loved them.

Thank you for the years we shared, for your love, your sacrifices and for the family we built together. Rest peacefully, my beloved wife. You will forever remain in my heart.

Until we meet again, may your soul rest in eternal peace.
